LIVE SYNC LAB

One release board on two devices.

Ada, the team lead, works on a laptop that syncs the Web and Mobile boards. Ben, a mobile engineer, has a phone that syncs only the Mobile board. Each device runs a Syncular client core with its own SQLite database, and the server in the middle orders every write into one commit log.

Guided tour

0/5
  1. 1
    Move a card

    Drag a card to another column on either device, or open it and pick a column. The move enters the commit log and reaches the other device.

    Next
  2. 2
    Go offline and move cards on both

    Switch the phone offline and move a Mobile card on it, then move a card on the laptop. The phone keeps its writes in its outbox.

    Open
  3. 3
    Reconnect and watch the replay

    Switch the phone online. Its outbox drains in order and the log records each replayed commit.

    Open
  4. 4
    Create a conflict and resolve it

    Press Simulate conflict. Both devices move one card offline; the laptop gets the server row back. Open the card and keep one side.

    Open
  5. 5
    Change the phone's scope

    Press Grant Web under the phone, then read the phone's Local SQL lens. Revoke it again and the Web rows leave the phone.
